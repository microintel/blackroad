Why BlackRoad exists

Your money lives in a dozen different places

A bank app for your salary, a broker app for stocks, a notebook for money you've lent a friend, a passbook for fixed deposits, a folder of gold receipts. None of them talk to each other, so nobody — including you — can answer a simple question: what am I actually worth right now?

The problem

Wealth is scattered across apps, notebooks and receipts, so there's no single, trustworthy view of income, assets and debts — and building one by hand is tedious enough that most people never do it.

Our vision

A world where anyone can open their phone and see their complete financial picture instantly — private by default, working offline, with no company standing between them and their own numbers.

Our mission

Give people one calm, honest place to track everything they own and owe — built simply enough to run entirely on their own device, and fast enough to check in seconds.

Under the hood

Built plain, on purpose

No heavy framework, no server holding your numbers. BlackRoad is intentionally simple today, with a clear path toward a more capable build.

How it's built today

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no framework yet.
  • Data is saved in your own browser's storage, so it stays on your device.
  • Sign-in checks your email against a securely hashed password — the real password is never stored.
  • A background service worker makes the app installable and lets it keep working offline.

Current limitations

  • Data stays on one device or browser — clearing it or switching devices loses it unless you export first.
  • No backend or database yet, so there's no automatic sync or cross-device backup.
  • No cloud-verified accounts — sign-up is a simple local system for now.
  • Not yet a unified React app — still separate HTML/JS pages per section.

Shared devices

One app, many people — each with their own numbers

BlackRoad can hold more than one person's data on the same browser or device. Every account is tied to the email address used to register, so no two people's entries ever mix — even if they're using the exact same computer.

Your email is the key

Everything you add — income, loans, gold, stocks — is stored under your email address. A second person can register on the same device with their own email, and their entries stay in a completely separate space.

Switching is just signing in

Anyone can sign out and sign back in with a different email to jump straight to that person's dashboard. There's no mixing or overwriting — each login only ever sees its own account's data.

Exporting is what makes it portable

Because all of this lives in one browser and nowhere else, exporting a backup file is essential — it's the only way to move an account to a new device, hand a shared computer back cleanly, or restore your numbers if something's cleared.

Frequently asked

Is my data stored online?

No — by default everything is stored locally in your browser. Cloud sync is on the roadmap but not available yet.

Can I use BlackRoad on more than one device?

Yes, manually for now: export a backup from Settings on one device and restore it on another. Automatic cross-device sync is coming with the planned cloud backup feature.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Anything not backed up will be lost, since there's no server copy yet. Export a backup regularly if this matters to you.

Does BlackRoad give financial advice?

No. It's a tracking and calculation tool — StepUp's projections, for example, are estimates for planning, not investment recommendations.
